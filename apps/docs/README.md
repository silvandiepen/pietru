# Pietru Documentation

Pietru is an authenticated mail gateway that brings all your transactional emails into one simple dashboard.

## What is Pietru?

Pietru sits between your application and email providers (Postmark, SendGrid, Mailgun, etc.), capturing every message that flows through. It gives you a unified inbox to inspect, search, and debug your product's emails without logging into multiple provider dashboards.

## Why Pietru?

- **One dashboard** for all your email providers
- **Capture** every sent and received message
- **Search and filter** across all projects
- **Team access** with role-based permissions
- **API-first** — integrate with your existing workflow
- **Mailing lists** — capture product update signups through the Pietru API
